## What I'm building

### openloops
**A framework to put an AI agent into production in minutes.** A shared `Agent` runtime with swappable `AgentLoop` implementations (plan and execute, ReAct, coder, customer support), multi-user by design, with MCP support and sub-agent orchestration. Apache-2.0.

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/openloops.xyz-6d28d9?style=flat-square&logo=safari&logoColor=white)](https://openloops.xyz)
[![npm](https://img.shields.io/npm/v/openloops?style=flat-square&logo=npm&color=cb3837)](https://www.npmjs.com/package/openloops)
[![Weekly downloads](https://img.shields.io/npm/dw/openloops?style=flat-square&color=6d28d9)](https://www.npmjs.com/package/openloops)
[![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-repo-0f172a?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/jbarcosdev/openloops)

### Sofi
**An AI agent for people and entrepreneurs who want their day handled.** It keeps track of your finances and calendar, summarizes your messages, runs analysis, and takes care of everyday tasks. Live in 107 countries.

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/sofi.cash-ff6b9d?style=flat-square&logo=safari&logoColor=white)](https://sofi.cash)
![Countries](https://img.shields.io/badge/countries-107-c06cf0?style=flat-square)

### iso-data
**Countries, currencies, languages and timezones in a single zero-dependency package.** Localized names through a `locale` option, timezone offsets for any date, and typed lookups by ISO code. Apache-2.0.

[![npm](https://img.shields.io/npm/v/iso-data?style=flat-square&logo=npm&color=cb3837)](https://www.npmjs.com/package/iso-data)
[![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-repo-0f172a?style=flat-square&logo=github)](https://github.com/jbarcosdev/iso-data)
